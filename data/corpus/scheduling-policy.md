# Booking, rescheduling and cancelling

Routine appointments can be booked through the patient portal or by calling the clinic
during working hours. When you book you will be asked for your date of birth and your
medical record number so the clinic can confirm it has the right record.

Primary care appointments are released four weeks ahead. Orthopaedic and imaging slots are
released two weeks ahead because those lists change more often. If nothing suitable is
showing, the clinic can add you to the short notice list.

To reschedule, cancel the existing appointment first and then book the new one. Giving at
least two working days notice lets the slot go to someone else. Two missed appointments
without notice in a twelve month period prompts a letter from the clinic.

Some bookings always need a human scheduler rather than self service. Those are:
appointments that need an interpreter, appointments that need transport, any booking where
the insurance or funding details do not match the record, any booking for a patient who is
already on an urgent pathway, and any request to see a specific named clinician.

If a booking fails in the system, the clinic does not leave it there. The request goes to
a scheduler who calls the patient back the same working day.

Imaging appointments that are missed are followed up by the imaging team. If you missed a
scan, expect a call offering you a new slot rather than having to chase it yourself.
