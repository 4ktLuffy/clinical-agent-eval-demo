# General clinical FAQ

Visiting hours on the general wards are 11am to 8pm every day. Intensive care and the
paediatric ward set their own hours and the ward clerk will tell you what they are when
you arrive. Two visitors are allowed at the bedside at any one time.

Prescription refills are requested through the patient portal or by asking the pharmacy
team at your next visit. A routine refill request is processed within two working days.
A refill for a controlled medicine needs a review by the prescribing clinician first, so
allow five working days. The care team will not change a dose over the phone.

If you need to reach your care team between appointments, send a message through the
patient portal. Messages are read during working hours, Monday to Friday. If something
cannot wait until the next working day, the correct route is the on-call service rather
than the portal.

If you miss an appointment, the clinic holds your referral open for eight weeks. Within
that window you can rebook at the same priority. After eight weeks the referral closes and
your general practitioner has to send a new one. Missed imaging appointments are followed
up by the imaging team directly, because scanner slots are released quickly.

Interpreting services are free and can be booked for any appointment. Ask when you book
and the clinic will arrange it for the language you prefer.

Test results are released to the patient portal once a clinician has reviewed them. That
review is what takes the time, not the laboratory. Results are not read out over the phone
by administrative staff.
